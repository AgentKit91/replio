# Roadmap / Explicitly Out of MVP

These items may influence extensibility but Codex must not implement them in the MVP unless the specification is formally revised.

## Phase 2 candidates

- Outlook inbox source;
- Replio-managed creator inbox / custom creator email;
- custom-domain email;
- richer contract upload/extraction/review;
- invoice generation;
- payment tracking/chasing;
- deliverable operations/renewal reminders beyond the data structure needed for Deal analysis;
- opportunity discovery / outreach;
- creator platform API enrichment beyond basic approved profile data;
- richer brand intelligence surfaces once data threshold exists;
- natural-language search;
- advanced automation.

## Future vision

- agency/team mode;
- shared workspaces;
- multiple creator identities/businesses;
- creator/brand marketplace;
- Brand Intelligence Graph;
- advanced reporting/data products;
- API/integrations ecosystem;
- media kit tools;
- accounting/tax integrations;
- Founder AI assistant.

## Explicitly rejected/bounded

- Creator Score;
- full inbox scanning as MVP trigger;
- random reply regeneration;
- raw model chain-of-thought storage/display;
- fake/pre-populated brand negotiation claims;
- user-facing AI credit counter as the primary experience;
- AI fine-tuning as an MVP dependency.
